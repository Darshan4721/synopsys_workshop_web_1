## 3. Institutional Lessons Learned
1. **Front-End Domain Precision**: Front-end VLSI workshops require distinct technical separation from back-end physical design (P&R/GDSII). Explicitly featuring VCS, Verdi, SpyGlass, and `dc_shell` builds high credibility with academic and industry audiences.
2. **Workstation Clarity**: Clear communication of 1:1 hardware availability and zero laptop prerequisites dramatically accelerates participant conversion.
3. **Typography Authority**: Pairing high-contrast editorial serifs for headlines with geometric grotesk for UI numbers and JetBrains Mono for EDA console scripts achieves a high-end agency appearance.
